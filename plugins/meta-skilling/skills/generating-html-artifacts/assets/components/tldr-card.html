TL;DR

A misconfigured connection pool in cfg-9a12 capped checkout concurrency at 4 and caused 47 minutes of elevated 502s. We rolled the config back, and recovery took 6 minutes once identified. No data was lost; 0.3% of checkouts in the window need a retry sweep.

  • Root cause: pool size env var overridden to the default by a config-schema rename.
  • Fix: rollback + a schema-validation check that now fails the deploy on unknown keys.
